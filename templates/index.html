How SOU Ride Works

Three effortless steps to travel smarter to Silver Oak University

1. Offer or Find a Ride

Students with 2-wheelers or cars publish their travel schedule and pickup points (Gota, Vaishno Devi, Chandkheda, etc.). Passengers search and select convenient rides.

2. Secure Seat Reservation

Drivers review requests and accept verified passengers. Real-time atomic seat tracking guarantees zero overbooking and automatic seat restoration if plans change.

3. Ride & Rate

Coordinate at campus gates via verified contacts. After the trip is completed, share honest ratings to keep the university network trustworthy and safe.
