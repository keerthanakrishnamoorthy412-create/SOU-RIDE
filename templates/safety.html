Silver Oak University Commute Code of Conduct

Follow these essential rules to ensure safe, comfortable journeys for everyone:

🪪
Verify Student ID

Always check each other's university enrollment ID card before getting into the vehicle.

📍
Public Campus Landmarks

Pick up passengers only at designated spots (Main Gate, Gate 2, Apple Lab, Gota BRTS stop).

⛑️
Protective Gear

Helmets are compulsory for two-wheeler rides on SG Highway. Seatbelts mandatory in cars.

🤝
Mutual Respect

Strict zero tolerance for rash driving, harassment, or substance consumption during campus commutes.

Report a Safety Concern or Incident

Reports are confidentially reviewed by the Silver Oak student transport cell & platform administrators.
